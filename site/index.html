Mohager Social Publisher

The tool Mohager uses to announce new versions of its apps on Mohager's own Facebook Page, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube channel and TikTok.

When a new app version is released, it drafts one post per platform in English and Arabic. Mohager reviews and approves each post before it is published. It posts only to Mohager's own accounts and collects no data from anyone.
